About the Academy

A small academy teaching the Quran and Arabic the way it has always been taught — one student, one teacher, live.

Who teaches here

The academy was founded by Miftah Uddin Roomi, a graduate of Jamia Darul Uloom Karachi. The teaching approach across every course follows the same classical method: one student, one teacher, reading aloud and being corrected in the moment.

That method is not a style choice. Quran instruction has always been oral and one-to-one, because the things that matter most — where a letter is formed in the mouth, how long a sound is held, whether a memorised portion is still intact — can only be assessed by someone listening.

What we believe about teaching

Accuracy before speed. A student who reads slowly and correctly is ahead of one who reads quickly with errors, because the second has habits to undo.

An honest pace. We set a daily portion a student can actually sustain rather than the largest one they can manage once. This matters most in Hifz, where an over-ambitious start is the usual reason memorisation is later abandoned.

No shame in the lesson. A child who comes to associate Quran lessons with being corrected harshly will resist them for years afterwards. Correction is constant here and it is never unkind.

How classes run

Every class is live, one to one, on video. You choose the days and times that fit your week, in your own timezone, and you keep the same tutor so that progress is tracked by someone who knows the student.

Male and female tutors are available. Families who prefer a female tutor for daughters or for sisters studying alone can request one at registration.

Before you commit to anything

The first class is free and it is a real lesson. The tutor assesses the student's level and you see how they teach. Nobody is asked to pay before that has happened.
