How it works

Four steps from here to your first lesson

  1. Choose your course

    Read the course page and see exactly what is covered, who it is for, and how long it usually takes.

  2. Message us on WhatsApp

    Tell us the student's age, the course, and the days and times that suit you. We will arrange a free trial class.

  3. Take the free trial and meet your tutor

    A real lesson, not a sales call. The tutor assesses the student's level and you both decide whether it is a fit.

  4. Register and begin

    Happy after the trial? Complete the registration form, confirm your schedule, and classes start.

Before you submit the form: please message us on WhatsApp to arrange your free trial class and meet your tutor. Once the trial is done and you are happy, complete the registration form and your classes begin.
