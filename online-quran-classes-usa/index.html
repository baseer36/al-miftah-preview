Online Quran Classes for Families in the USA

Live one-to-one classes scheduled around American school hours and working days, with a free trial before you pay.

Classes in your timezone

Classes run on Eastern, Central, Mountain and Pacific time — before school, after school, or late evening for adults.

Families across the United States study with us, and the most popular slots are weekday evenings US time, which is early morning in Pakistan.

What families in the USA usually start with

Children who have not read Arabic before begin with the Noorani Qaida, then move to Nazra. Children who already read start with Nazra or Tajweed. Adults — including reverts — most often begin with the Prayer Guide alongside the Qaida, because the prayer is the immediate need.

Fees in US dollars

Fees start at $22 a month for two classes a week and run to $60 for seven. Hifz and Arabic start at $25. The full table is on the fees page.

Before you pay anything

The first class is free. You meet the tutor, the tutor assesses the student, and you decide afterwards.
