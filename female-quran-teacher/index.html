Female Quran Teachers for Sisters and Girls

If you would prefer a female tutor for your daughter, or for yourself, ask for one — it is a normal request and it changes nothing else about the course.

Why families ask for a female tutor

For many families it is simply what makes a daughter comfortable enough to speak, make mistakes and be corrected — which is the whole mechanism by which Quran learning works. For adult sisters studying alone it is often a matter of preference or of what their household is comfortable with.

Neither reason needs explaining to us. Say at registration that you would like a female tutor and that is what you get.

Which courses female tutors teach

All of them: Noorani Qaida, Nazra Quran, Tajweed, Hifz, Tafseer, Basic Fiqh, the Prayer Guide, Islamic Duas and Arabic Language.

How to request one

Mention it when you first message on WhatsApp, or choose Female tutor on the registration form. The free trial class will be with a female tutor, so you are deciding with the actual teacher in front of you rather than on a promise.

Classes for adult sisters

Adult students are welcome on every course, including complete beginners. A number of students start with the Noorani Qaida as adults, and it is a very ordinary place to begin.

Questions

Common questions

Can I request a female Quran teacher for my daughter?

Yes. Mention it when you message us or select it on the registration form, and your free trial class will be with a female tutor.

Do female tutors teach Hifz as well?

Yes. Female tutors teach every course offered, including Hifz and Arabic.

Can an adult sister take classes alone?

Yes. Adult students are welcome on every course, at any level, including complete beginners.

Is the trial class with the same tutor who will teach afterwards?

That is the intention — the trial exists so the student and the tutor can meet before anything is agreed.

Meet the tutor, see how a class runs, and decide afterwards. There is no payment and no obligation.
