Basic Fiqh

Basic Fiqh Course Online

Fiqh is the practical law of worship — how to purify, how to pray, when to fast, what to pay. This course covers what a Muslim needs to practise correctly, without turning into a legal seminar.

Any Age 10 and up, and adults 4–8 months From $22 / month

What you will be able to do

  • Perform wudu and ghusl correctly and know what invalidates them
  • Know the conditions, pillars and invalidators of salah
  • Understand the rulings of fasting, including the concessions
  • Calculate zakat on the common categories of wealth
  • Know the outline of hajj and umrah before performing either

The syllabus

What the course covers, in order

  1. Taharah — purification

    Wudu, ghusl, tayammum, and the rulings around what is and is not pure.

  2. Salah

    Conditions, pillars, what is obligatory and what is sunnah, the prostration of forgetfulness, and the prayer of the traveller.

  3. Sawm — fasting

    What breaks the fast, what does not, and the legitimate concessions.

  4. Zakat

    Nisab, the categories of wealth, who receives it, and how to calculate it.

  5. Hajj and Umrah

    The rites in order, with the common mistakes.

What Fiqh is

Fiqh is the practical law of worship: how to purify yourself, how to pray, how to fast, how to give zakat, how to perform Hajj. It turns belief into action. Teaching here follows the Hanafi school, with differences of opinion noted where they are significant.
