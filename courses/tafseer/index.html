What Tafseer is

Tafseer is the explanation of the Quran: what a verse meant when it was revealed, what its words carry in Arabic, and what it asks of the reader. It is different from a translation, which only swaps the language.

How a verse is explained

  1. 1
    The context

    When and why the passage was revealed, and what comes before and after it.

  2. 2
    The language

    What the Arabic words mean, and why that word was chosen.

  3. 3
    The classical commentary

    What the scholars of Tafseer have said, in plain language.

  4. 4
    The lesson

    What the verse asks of a reader today.

Questions

Common questions about this course

Do I need Arabic for Tafseer?

No. Classes are taught in English (or Urdu on request) and the Arabic is explained as it comes.

Which Tafseer do you teach from?

The classical works of Tafseer, explained in plain language. Specific texts are agreed with the student before the course begins.

Is this suitable for a new Muslim?

Yes — it is often the most useful course to start with alongside reading.
