Online Arabic Language Course

Most people who read the Quran do not understand it. This course closes that gap — building Arabic from the alphabet through grammar to the point where the meaning of a verse arrives without a translation beside it.

What you will be able to do

  • Read and write Arabic script fluently
  • Build a working vocabulary of the words most common in the Quran
  • Understand nahw (syntax) and sarf (morphology) at a functional level
  • Parse a Quranic sentence and see why it means what it means
  • Follow a passage of the Quran without leaning on the translation

The syllabus

What the course covers, in order

  1. Script and sound

    Reading and writing the alphabet, for students who need it.

  2. Core vocabulary

    The high-frequency words of the Quran first — a small list covers a surprising proportion of the text.

  3. Sarf — word forms

    How Arabic builds words from roots, which is what makes vocabulary multiply instead of accumulate.

  4. Nahw — sentence structure

    Cases, the verbal sentence, the nominal sentence, and how meaning is carried by form.

  5. Reading real Arabic

    Applying all of it to actual verses, then to longer passages.

  6. Conversational Arabic

    Optional, for students who want to speak as well as read.

How Arabic works

Most Arabic words are built from a root of three letters, and every word in the family shares its core meaning. Seeing the root is what lets a student guess an unfamiliar word, and it is one of the first habits the course builds.

Questions

Common questions about this course

Is this Modern Standard Arabic or Quranic Arabic?

The focus is classical and Quranic Arabic. The grammar overlaps heavily with Modern Standard Arabic, and conversational practice can be added on request.

How long before I can understand the Quran?

Students typically begin recognising structure within months and reading short passages without translation within one to two years. Pace depends on days per week and study between classes.

Do I need to be able to read Arabic already?

No. The course begins at the script if that is where you are.
