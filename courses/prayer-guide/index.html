Prayer Guide

Learn to Pray Salah — Step by Step

This is the shortest course on the site and often the most urgently needed. It teaches the prayer from beginning to end — every position, every word, in the right order — until it can be performed without prompting.

Complete beginner welcome Age 5 and up, and adults 6–10 weeks From $22 / month

What you will be able to do

  • Perform wudu correctly and know what breaks it
  • Pray all five daily prayers with the correct number of rak'ahs
  • Recite the words of each position accurately in Arabic
  • Understand what each phrase of the prayer means
  • Know what to do when something is missed or forgotten

The syllabus

What the course covers, in order

  1. Wudu, step by step

    The sequence, the obligatory parts, and what invalidates it.

  2. The positions of the prayer

    Qiyam, ruku, sujood and jalsa, demonstrated and corrected on camera.

  3. The words of the prayer

    Takbir, Al-Fatihah, the tasbeeh of ruku and sujood, tashahhud and the salutations — taught to accurate pronunciation.

  4. The five prayers

    Times, rak'ah counts, and which parts are said aloud.

  5. When something goes wrong

    Sujood as-sahw, making up a missed prayer, and praying while travelling or unwell.

Questions

Common questions about this course

I have never prayed before. Is this course for me?

Yes. It assumes nothing and begins at wudu.

Can my child learn salah online?

Yes. The positions are demonstrated on camera and the child performs them back, which is how the tutor sees and corrects what a parent might miss.

Do I need to understand Arabic?

No. You learn to say the words correctly and are taught what each one means.
