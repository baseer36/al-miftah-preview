Tajweed

Online Tajweed Classes

Tajweed is the set of rules that govern how the Quran is pronounced — where each letter is made in the mouth, how long a sound is held, and what happens when certain letters meet. It cannot be learned from a book alone, because nobody can hear their own mistakes.

The syllabus

What the course covers, in order

  1. Makharij — the articulation points

    The 17 points in the throat, tongue and lips where the Arabic letters are formed, practised letter by letter.

  2. Sifaat — the qualities of the letters

    What distinguishes letters that share an articulation point.

  3. Noon saakin and tanween

    Izhar, idgham, iqlab and ikhfa, taught where they appear in real verses.

  4. Meem saakin

    The three rules, and the common errors in each.

  5. Madd — the lengths

    Natural madd and its branches, and holding each for its correct count.

  6. Qalqalah, ghunnah and the heavy letters

    The finishing qualities that separate a correct recitation from a careful one.

What Tajweed is

Tajweed is the set of rules that govern how the Quran is pronounced: where each letter is formed, how long a sound is held, and what happens when letters meet. It is the difference between reading the words and reciting them correctly.

Questions

Common questions about this course

Do I need Tajweed if I can already read?

Reading and reciting correctly are different skills. Most people who learned to read by imitation carry small, consistent errors they cannot hear.

Can Tajweed be self-taught from videos?

The rules can be understood from videos. The pronunciation cannot be corrected by them, because correction requires someone listening to you.

How long does Tajweed take?

Six to twelve months for the full set of rules, with the makharij taking the longest because they are physical habits, not information.
