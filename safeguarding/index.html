Safeguarding Policy

Most of our students are children. This policy sets out how we keep classes safe and what you should expect from a tutor.

Our commitment

The welfare of a child comes before every other consideration, including the lesson. Any concern is acted on rather than managed quietly.

How classes are run

Classes take place on video, at an agreed time, between one tutor and one student.

Parents are welcome to be present, to watch, and to see the class at any time without notice. No part of a lesson is private from a parent.

We ask that children attend lessons in a shared part of the home rather than behind a closed door.

What tutors may and may not do

Tutors communicate with families through the academy's own channels. A tutor must not contact a child privately on any platform, or arrange a class outside the agreed arrangement.

Tutors must not ask a child for personal information beyond what the lesson requires, must not ask a child to keep anything secret from a parent, and must not offer or request gifts, money or private contact.

Discipline is verbal, calm and about the work. Shouting, humiliation and threats have no place in a lesson and are grounds for removing a tutor.

Recording

Tutors do not record lessons. A parent who wishes to record a child's lesson for revision should tell the tutor in advance.

Raising a concern

If anything about a tutor's conduct worries you — however small, and whether or not you are sure — tell us. Contact the academy's safeguarding lead at admin@almiftahquranacademy.com or message the academy on WhatsApp.

We will respond, investigate, and tell you what we have done. A tutor will be suspended from teaching while a serious concern is looked into.

If you believe a child is at immediate risk, contact your local police or child protection service first. Do not wait for us.

Review
