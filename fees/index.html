What the fee includes

  • Live one-to-one classes — one student with one tutor, never a group
  • A fixed weekly schedule in your own timezone
  • A free trial class before you pay anything
  • Progress feedback for parents
  • Male or female tutor, as you prefer

Things worth knowing

No registration fee. You pay for classes and nothing else.

Fees are per student, per month, in US dollars.

Changing your schedule is fine — tell us before the next month begins and we will move you to a different plan.

Sibling and family arrangements are available. Ask when you register and we will confirm what applies to you.

About fees and payment

Do I pay for the trial class?

No. The trial class is free and there is no obligation to continue afterwards.

Yes. Let us know before the next month begins and we will move you.

Is there a discount for more than one child?

Family arrangements are available. Ask when you register and we will tell you exactly what applies to your situation.

Tell us in advance and we will pause your schedule rather than charging for classes you will not take.
