Refund Policy

Our approach is simple: you should not pay for classes you have not had.

The free trial

The trial class is free. Nothing is charged before it and there is no obligation afterwards.

Unused classes

If you stop classes part way through a month, we refund the unused portion of that month's fee, or carry it forward to the next month — whichever you prefer.

Classes we cancel

If a tutor is unable to take a scheduled class, we reschedule it. If it cannot be rescheduled, that class is refunded or credited.

Classes you miss

Classes missed with notice are rescheduled where the timetable allows. Classes missed without notice are not normally refunded, because the tutor's time was held for the student.

How to request a refund

Message us on WhatsApp or email info@miftahquranacademy.com with the student's name and the month concerned. [Temporary: refund processing time to be confirmed.]
